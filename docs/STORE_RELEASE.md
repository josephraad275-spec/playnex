# Android and iOS release checklist

Status: evaluation only. No native project, signing identity, binary, store listing, developer account purchase or publication is claimed.

Policy review status: **not completed**. Before native implementation, review current official Apple App Review Guidelines and Google Play Developer Policies. Record date, URLs, relevant sections and decisions covering remote game delivery, minimum functionality, billing, subscriptions, children/families, data collection/deletion and moderation.

After a stable browser beta, evaluate Capacitor with native navigation/back handling, safe areas, deep links/app links, OS share sheet, offline recovery and secure credential storage. Provide original icons, splash assets, accessibility labels and truthful screenshots from working builds. Validate on physical Android and iOS devices.

Founder authorization gates: app IDs and legal publisher identity, developer accounts and fees, privacy/support URLs, age/region availability, signing access, store declarations and final submission. Do not ask the founder to send signing keys or passwords in chat. iOS builds/signing require supported macOS tooling.

Complete release build configuration, permissions minimization, account deletion flow, privacy disclosures/manifests, encryption/export declarations, crash monitoring consent, rollback/support process, release notes and store review credentials if appropriate. A PWA install is not an App Store or Google Play download. Publication is complete only when the store confirms availability.
